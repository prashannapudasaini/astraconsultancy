# Content evidence and scope
Reviewed 28 September 2026.

KIEC (https://kiec.edu.np/) informed content coverage: destination information, preparation, application process and resources. No KIEC testimonials, experience figures, certifications, partnerships or marketing copy are represented as ASTRA facts.

ASTRA identity, address, contact details, three core services and five destination labels come from the user-supplied visiting card and logo. Specific programmes, accreditation, fees, staff biographies, partnerships and student outcomes remain unverified and are not asserted.

Official sources are mapped in src/content.jsx and shown beside the relevant guidance:
- GOV.UK: Student visa documents, course and CAS requirements.
- Immigration New Zealand: Fee Paying Student Visa.
- NIIED / Study in Korea: student visa categories and documentation.
- JASSO / Study in Japan: examinations and academic fees.
- European Commission: study planning and Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters.
- Government of Nepal: official NOC portal.
- IELTS and Pearson: Academic test descriptions.

Planning questions and checklists are editorial recommendations, not universal admissions or visa requirements. No predictions or promises of eligibility, visas, work rights, scholarships or permanent residence are made.

The enquiry form creates a local mailto draft and does not store or transmit form entries to a site backend. Source links do not imply institutional partnership or endorsement.
